◆ SampleInterpolator

using gs::animation::SampleInterpolator = typedef std::function<void(const std::span<float>, const std::span<float>, std::span<float>, float)>

Callback type for custom multi-channel interpolation.

Receives left-hand-side values, right-hand-side values, and writes interpolated results into the output span. The float parameter is the normalized progress between the two keyframes in [0.0, 1.0].

Parameters
lhsSpan of left keyframe values.
rhsSpan of right keyframe values.
outSpan to write interpolated results into.
tInterpolation factor in [0.0, 1.0].
