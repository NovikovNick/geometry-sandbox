Detailed Description

Lightweight handle for controlling a playing animation instance.

Acts as a "smart reference" to an active animation, hiding the internal structure of the animation manager and raw identifier access from client code. Binds together a pointer to the owning gs::animation::IManager and a unique gs::animation::InstanceId.
